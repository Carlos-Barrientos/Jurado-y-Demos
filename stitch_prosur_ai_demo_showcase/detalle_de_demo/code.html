About this Demo

This demonstration highlights our new AI-driven computer vision system designed for real-time quality control on the Prosur manufacturing line. By leveraging a custom convolutional neural network trained on thousands of defect samples, the system can identify microscopic anomalies in product packaging with 99.8% accuracy.

The model runs on edge devices directly at the inspection stations, ensuring zero latency and allowing for immediate mechanical sorting of defective units before they proceed to shipping.
